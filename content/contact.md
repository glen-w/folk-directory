To propose a **new or updated listing**, use the structured form on [Submit / update a listing](/submit/).

If you organise or run a listed event, you can [claim your listing](/claim/) from the event’s own page so we can match future organiser tools to the right record. Claims are reviewed by email and do not automatically change the public page.

For other questions, email [glen.w.wright@gmail.com](mailto:glen.w.wright@gmail.com).

On each listing page you can also use **Suggest an update** to open a pre-addressed email with the listing id in the subject line.
