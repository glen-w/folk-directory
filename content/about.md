**2005** — The Folk Directory launched in June 2005 with just over 160 entries.

**2006** — Site grew to 400+ listings and over 1000 unique monthly visits. Received Arts Council funding.

**2010** — Disaster! Web address squatted and backup hard disk failure.

**2011** — Site remade.

**2018** — Site quietly slipped away.

**2026** — Site back! Completely remade on folkdirectory.co.uk with the preserved 2018 UK listings, an expanded set of clubs, sessions and festivals, and a map of venues. [Get in touch](/contact) with thoughts, feedback and comments.
