A mixed genre event, with a folk element for you to enjoy. More than 25 venues and 250 artists (!) to enjoy. Worcester Music Festival are pleased to announce we will be working alongside Acorns Children's Hospice this year, with the money raised by the festival going towards Acorns' music therapy scheme.
