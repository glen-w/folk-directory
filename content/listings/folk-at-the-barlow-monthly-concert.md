Folk at the Barlow is a not-for-profit folk club run by unpaid volunteers, with the aim of encouraging people of all ages and backgrounds to enjoy and participate in folk music and song. The club is based at The Barlow, a venue with a stage, state-of-the-art sound and lighting, and a separate bar serving local ale.

A singaround is held on the first Friday of each month in the Reading Room, and a concert takes place on the third Friday of each month, doors opening at 7:30pm with the concert starting at 8:00pm. Concert tickets are £12.50; children aged 16 and under are free and students aged 21 and under pay half price (£6.25). The singaround is free, with a whip-round to cover room hire.

The concert format typically features a 40-minute support act followed by two 45-minute sets from the main act, with an interval for the raffle and a visit to the bar. A house band plays while the audience checks in, and visitors are welcome to bring an instrument and join in. The singaround welcomes tunes, songs, monologues, poems, and jokes, with participants going round the room in a variety of styles and genres.
