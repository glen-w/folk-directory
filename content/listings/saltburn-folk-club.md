Saltburn Folk Club meets at Saltburn House, Marine Parade, Saltburn by the Sea. Monday night sessions open at 6:30 for a 7:00 start and run until 10:00. Zoom sessions are also held on Wednesdays and Saturdays.

The club is an informal gathering with a committee member acting as MC. All are welcome to singarounds, acoustic music performances (traditional or contemporary, accompanied or unaccompanied), and poetry readings. Everyone who wishes to perform is invited to do so, and there is no pressure to take the floor. After a short break, a second round of performances is offered, sometimes facilitated through raffle tickets.

An annual membership fee of £3 runs for 12 months from 1 October. Members' admission is £2, which includes a raffle ticket; non-members pay £3. Concerts are held in Saltburn Community Hall, with tickets available to members at £8 (maximum two) and to non-members at £12.
