Our definition

“But I don’t like folk music ...”  Don’t worry.

Our definition of folk music is broad and takes in everything from a traditional English ballad to contemporary Americana.

Previous acts have  included  Eilen Jewell...Michael Chapman...Po’ Girl …Martha Tilston. Surely something for everyone.

We are a non-profit making organisation, run as a club dedicated to putting  on high quality live acoustic music.

Membership is £10 for 12 months and members receive a discount on Folk Night tickets.
