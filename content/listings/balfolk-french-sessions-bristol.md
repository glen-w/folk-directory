French and Breton tune sessions with dancing, held in two locations. The Bath session takes place on the first Monday of the month at 8pm in the bar of the Royal Oak pub, Lower Bristol Road, BA2 3BW. The Bristol session takes place on the third Sunday of the month at 7.30pm, with a dance workshop at 6.45pm, in the upstairs room of Charlie's Bar at The Merchant of Cotham, 31–35 Cotham Hill, Bristol BS6 6JY. Both sessions are free to attend; visitors are asked to support the venue.

The sessions feature traditional and new French and Breton tunes, danced in couples or in lines without partners. Instruments typically include bagpipes, hurdy-gurdy, accordions, melodeons, concertina, fiddles, and whistles. The Bath session is held in the bar, with some space for dancing, while the Bristol session has a dedicated upstairs room with more room for dancing.

The sessions are around 30 years old, originally started in Bristol by Andy Letcher.
