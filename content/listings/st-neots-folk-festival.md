The St Neots Folk Festival is an annual event associated with the St Neots Folk Club, which has been running since 1965. The club meets on Tuesday evenings from 7:30pm to 9:45pm at The Eatons, 18 The Maltings, Eaton Socon, PE19 8ES, with parking available nearby.

The club hosts guest artist nights most weeks, Round The Room open sessions on the last Tuesday of each month (except August), and occasional Open Stage and Showcase nights. Entry to artist nights is usually £8 for members and £10 for non-members; Round The Room evenings are £5. Membership is £20 per year, running from September to August. The club maintains a friendly, welcoming atmosphere and encourages anyone to attend.
