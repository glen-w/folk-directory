Kiveton Park Folk Club is a community music venue known for its welcoming atmosphere and dedication to preserving folk heritage. The club hosts weekly or monthly sessions featuring a wide range of folk styles, from traditional Celtic ballads and Appalachian songs to contemporary singer-songwriter pieces and world folk influences. Events are open to music lovers of all ages, with seasoned performers sharing the stage alongside emerging artists.

The club maintains an open-mic culture, offering a supportive, non-competitive environment for new performers to gain confidence and experiment with material, as well as a space for experienced musicians to connect with new listeners and collaborate. Beyond regular sessions, the club hosts workshops, presentations, and themed events that explore the origins and meanings behind classic songs, instruments, and storytelling techniques.

Kiveton Park Folk Club also partners with local organisations, libraries, and cultural groups to host special events, charity concerts, and seasonal festivals, and offers musical programmes to schools. The atmosphere is warm and intimate, with attendees chatting between sets, sharing stories, and exchanging musical ideas.
