Black Fen Folk Club is a folk and roots music venue in Cambridge, holding an event every Sunday. The club's open stages provide the opportunity for anyone to play acoustic music to an attentive audience. Singers, instrumentalists, and dancers from all musical traditions and at all levels are welcome, as are non-performing listeners.

The club also runs a programme of guest nights bringing varied roots music to the city.
