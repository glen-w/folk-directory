Unicorn Ceilidhs are public dances with a good live band and caller. Couples, singles and groups are all welcome. There's a proper sprung dance floor, a well-stocked Real Ale bar, tables to sit at - and a great atmosphere!

See our website for details, or ring Alan on the number below (work hours)for a chat.
