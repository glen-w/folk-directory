We are a small 3-day festival based in a rural location on the Isle of Wight. FolkStation features music, song and dance as its programme offering a great family friendly and relaxed festival with great camping and something for everyone to enjoy. We endeavour to provide a wide range of folk music from traditional to contemporary. 

We provide a full range of on-site facilities including car parking, food, a real ale bar, local craft, 2 sound stages, a club tent and a range of entertainments for children as well.

We also can get you cheap deals on ferry travel to the Island.
