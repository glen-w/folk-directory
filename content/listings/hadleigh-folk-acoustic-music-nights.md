Hadleigh Folk & Acoustic Music Nights are held at The Ansell Centre, Market Place, Hadleigh IP7 5DL, normally on the first Friday of the month from September to June. The club features folk music in the broadest sense, encompassing traditional and contemporary folk alongside hints of pop, country, jazz, and classical. Doors open at 7.45 and the music begins at 8.00.

Each evening starts with an informal short floor spot, bookable in advance, followed by a 30-minute featured guest performance and a 60-minute main guest set. Occasionally two joint main guests share the slot at 45 minutes each. Evenings finish around 10.00. Entry is £8.00 with no advance tickets. There is no bar at the venue, but visitors are welcome to bring their own drinks. Free parking is available within easy walking distance.

The club also hosts an annual French weekend of music and dance. Organisers are Simon and Val Haines.
