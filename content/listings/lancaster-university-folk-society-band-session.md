Lancaster University Folk Music and Dance Society, formed in November 2014, holds weekly dance sessions, folk song and tune sessions, and music sessions. All are open to anyone, no partner or experience required, and are free after joining the society. The group is friendly and welcoming to all dancers and musicians.

Dance sessions (Red Rose Morris) take place on Mondays at 6pm in the Brandrigg Room, BHF. Music and song sessions take place on Fridays at 6pm in the Bowland Bar. Out of term time, dance sessions move to various venues, while music and song sessions are not currently held.
