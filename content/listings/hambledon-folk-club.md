There are guest performers most months, usually acoustic, covering a wide range of styles. Members of the club get a discount on guest nights. Membership costs just £3 per
year.

There are floorspots prior to the monthly guest, and an occasional Anything Goes evening.

Visitors are always welcome, be they singers, musicians or
listeners.

It's a friendly club. We bring drinks in from the Vine or bring our own. The club is a non smoking venue.
