The Glasgow Ballad Workshop was founded in 2010 by Anne Neilson, Gordeanna McCulloch and Ronnie Clark as a response to the under-representation of Scottish singers using traditional music and singing style at some of Scotland's major events. The group aims to demystify the Child Ballad and show that these are songs meant to be sung — traditional material that can be worked with to bring out its relevance in the 21st century. The Workshop also draws heavily on the wider base of Traditional Scottish Songs.

Members include Aileen Carr, Cy Laurie and Barbara Dymock, among others. The core of the Workshop is people coming together to sing, discuss, interpret and learn. Events have featured interpreters and singers of traditional folk song and ballad music from Scotland, Ireland, England and the USA.

The Glasgow Ballad Workshop meets on the last Sunday of every month, with the new season beginning in late September.
