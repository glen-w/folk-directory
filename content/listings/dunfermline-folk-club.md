Dunfermline Folk Club meets almost every Wednesday at The Glen Tavern, Dunfermline, though some events may take place in a different venue. The first Dunfermline Folk Club was founded in 1961, and the current club has met since 1994. The club supports all with an interest in folk music, giving performers and listeners the chance to make and enjoy the music in an informal, participatory setting. All are welcome.

Membership offers the chance to help shape the club's direction and includes £2.00 off the ticket price on guest nights. Visitors consistently note the warm welcome and energetic, engaging atmosphere.
