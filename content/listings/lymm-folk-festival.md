Formerly the Railway Folk Club, the group meets every Thursday at 8:30 pm. Following the Railway Hotel's closure in July 2007 and its destruction by fire in November 2011, the club relocated to the Spread Eagle in Lymm Centre, with occasional concerts at Lymm Rugby Club or the Saracen's Head. Bernard Cromarty handles tickets and enquiries. The club also runs the Kerry Smith Memorial Songwriting Competition.
