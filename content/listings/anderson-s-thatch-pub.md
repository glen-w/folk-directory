The Thatch is a traditional pub near Carrick-on-Shannon where music is central to the atmosphere. Sessions are lively and intimate, with traditional musicians from across Ireland visiting regularly to join locals for reels, songs, poetry, and spoken word. The venue has a long tradition of storytelling and offers space for acoustic sessions.

Newcomers to sessions are welcome; the crowd is a mix of locals and visitors, and the atmosphere is relaxed and old-world. The Thatch is also available for private hire for parties and special occasions, including after-wedding celebrations, birthdays, communions, and family gatherings, with live music or a session where possible.
