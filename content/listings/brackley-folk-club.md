Brackley Folk Club Brackley Folk Club meets every Sunday in the Greyhound, has regular guest nights, welcomes any music style, and has strong connections with the Brackley Morris. It also runs free folk day every June. Contact Don on 01280 840219, or don@brackleyfolkclub.org
