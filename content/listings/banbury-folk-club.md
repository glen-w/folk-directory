Banbury Folk Club meets on Wednesdays at 8.00pm at The Cromwell Lodge Hotel, North Bar St, OX16 0TB. Ample parking is available around the area.

Admission is £8 for regular nights; Special Guest Nights are priced as advertised. Payment is by cash on the door or by bank transfer (details available from the club). An ATM is approximately 200 yards from the venue. Floor spots of around 30 minutes are available on club guest nights, excluding Special Guest nights.
