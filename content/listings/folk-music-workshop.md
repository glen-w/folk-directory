Salford Folk Music Workshop, Salford, Greater Manchester. A group of people who enjoy playing folk music together. Members range in ability from music teachers to very amateur players. The group plays a variety of instruments — accordions, fiddles, guitars, keyboards, whistles, mandolins, bass — and any instrument is welcome.
