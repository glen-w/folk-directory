Parkgate Folk Club started in its present format in January 1994 in the Parkgate Hotel. In March 2000 the hotel closed for business and the club moved to The Boathouse.

The club is open on the last Thursday of each month, except December when it runs on the Thursday preceding the Christmas holiday. Occasionally extra nights are put on in the middle of the month to fit in with a particular artist's tour dates.

The club books international and national artists. The evenings are presented by either Brass Tacks or Eddie Morris and Bob Burrill, the resident singers, who start the evening with a 30-minute set before introducing the guest artist. Dina, Pat, Joyce, and Anne, the rest of the team, who work hard to help with the running of the club, are also there to ensure a warm welcome.
