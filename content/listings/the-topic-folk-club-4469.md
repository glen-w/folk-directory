The Topic Folk Club, established in 1956, meets at The Castle, 20 Grattan Road, Bradford BD1 2LU. The regular Thursday programme alternates between guest acts and a Singers and Musicians open mic, where anyone can sing a song, play a tune, or simply listen. Entry for the open mic is £1, with doors opening at 7pm.

The pub has two entrances: a lower door with steps on Barry Street and a ground-floor door on Grattan Road. On guest act nights the Grattan Road entrance should be used, as the other is locked. On-street parking is available on Barry Street and Grattan Road, with additional spaces on Fulton Street, a nearby turning off Grattan Road.

The club maintains a safeguarding policy and, to mark Bradford City of Culture in 2025, compiled "Songs With A Bradford Accent," a collection of 62 folk songs about the Bradford district. 2026 is the club's 70th anniversary year.
