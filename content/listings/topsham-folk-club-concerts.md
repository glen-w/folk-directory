Topsham Folk Club, now in its fifth decade, is one of the most active folk clubs in the UK, rooted in Topsham's seaport location. The club draws on a wide range of musical influences.

Monthly concerts take place on Sunday evenings at Matthews Hall, Topsham, with doors opening at 7:30 PM and the concert running from 8:00 PM to 10:30 PM. Tickets are available at singers' nights, at the Matthews Hall café (card only), or online. Youth (under 26) concessions are available.

Singers' nights are held every Tuesday (except August), usually at the Globe Inn on Fore Street. The evening begins with warm-up tunes from around 7:30 PM, with singers performing from 7:45 PM. Performers book a slot with the compere by 7:45 PM and typically take two songs or tunes in a 10-minute slot. Non-performers are welcome.

Instrumental sessions are held on the first Monday of each month at the Bridge Inn, Topsham, from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM. Instrumentalists of all levels are welcome to join in.
