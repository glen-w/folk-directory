Folk Acoustic & Blues Aylesbury (FAB) meets on the first and third Friday of each month from 7.30pm to 10.30pm. The club is open to all acoustic musicians and singers playing folk, blues, and related genres; all abilities are welcome. Some sessions are sing-arounds, where participants sit in a circle and take turns to perform, with the chance to play or sing together. Other sessions are feature nights, on which a guest performer is booked for a thirty-minute set in each half, with floor spots still available. The room is arranged with the audience facing the performance area on these nights, and the club aims to host feature nights every few weeks.

FAB meets at the Fairford Leys Centre, Hampden Square, Aylesbury HP19 7HT. There is parking for a few vehicles outside the venue, and a free car park next to the Co-op nearby. The hall has good acoustics and ample seating. As the venue does not have a bar, participants are asked to bring their own refreshments. The entrance fee is a flat £5, whether attending as a performer or audience member, and regardless of whether the session is a sing-around or a feature night.
