The Room at the Top Folk Club meets on Monday nights at The Red Lion, Ash Brow, Newburgh, WN8 7NF. The club runs from 8:15pm and finishes at 11:00pm sharp. Performers sing two songs each, with a break around 9:20; depending on the number of performers there is usually time for a second round of one song each. At 10:20 the raffle is drawn and those wishing to leave early are free to go. From 10:30pm a session is held for those who prefer to play along or learn songs rather than perform, with help offered on chords or playing if needed.

All nights are Singers Nights, and singers, poets, and readers are welcome to have a go at any time. All styles of acoustic music are encouraged. Admission is £1 for all, which includes a raffle ticket for a bottle of wine.

For more information, contact Mark or Maggi. More details of other folk clubs in the North West can be found at www.folknorthwest.co.uk.
