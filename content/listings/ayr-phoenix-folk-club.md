Ayr Phoenix Folk Club was established in November 2016. The club holds its main Sunday session on the first Sunday of each month at Belleisle Conservatory, Doonfoot Road, Ayr KA7 4DU, from 1 to 3 pm. A second Sunday afternoon session at Crumbs and Cocktails, 18 Smith Street, Ayr KA7 1TF, also runs from 1 to 3 pm, though this is currently on hold until further notice. Guest concert nights are held at The Holy Trinity Church Hall, 25 Fullarton Street, Ayr KA7 1UB, with doors opening at 7.15 pm.
