Over 500 events in one week, with venues all over Broadstairs, including the Concert Tent, Pavilion on the Sands, Sailing Club, Bandstand, Jetty and many more. A real ale tent sits next to the concert marquee; free music in pubs as well as sessions. Festival campsite available for season ticket holders.
