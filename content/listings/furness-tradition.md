Furness Tradition is a volunteer-run organisation promoting folk music and related arts and crafts in South Cumbria. Its mission is to keep local music and traditions alive while also playing and promoting a wide range of music across different traditions. The weekly session is usually held at The Hope and Anchor pub in Ulverston on Tuesday nights from 8:30 pm.

Furness Tradition routinely takes photos and video of its events; attendance implies consent. Full details are available in the Photography and Video Policy on the organisation's website.
