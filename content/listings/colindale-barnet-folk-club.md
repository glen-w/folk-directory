Colindale Barnet Folk Club meets at the Bull Theatre, Chipping Barnet, mostly on the third Friday of the month. The evening runs from 8:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m.

The first hour features four floor spots, each playing for approximately 12 minutes; performers are advised to book in advance as spots are usually taken before the evening begins. The second hour is devoted to the headline act. Admission is £10 on the night, or tickets may be purchased in advance via ticketsource.co.uk/thebulltheatre when made available by the theatre.

On folk club nights, visitors should enter via the side entrance (the red gates on the right side of the building). The club is politically neutral and is run for the love of folk music rather than political commentary.
