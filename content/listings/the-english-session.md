As far as we know, still the only weekly English Folk Music Session in London. Mainly traditional English Dance Tunes in sets with a few songs later on. Free buffet, usually a free drink for participants (Real Ale available). Totally acoustic. Has been known to finish quite late!

Buses 177, 180, 188, 199, 286, 386 DLR (Cutty Sark nearly opposite) and Greenwich Mainline Railway station (5 mins. You can park your car outside.
