The Howwood Inn , Main Street, Howwood, west Renfrewshire (a short journey from Glasgow City Centre) The Howwood Inn is the oldest inn in Renfrewshire and one of the oldest in Scotland. It has a unique character with a lively atmosphere and a wide range of roots based music.

A friendly session, a mix of traditional tunes and whatever songs you choose to give us. Set around a roaring fire in a local village pub. All welcome.
