Open acoustic session: just turn up with (or without) an instrument and you can sing a song or play a tune. As well as guitarists and singers, we tend to get (and encourage) fiddle players, mandolin players, melodeon players, harmonica players, and percussionists. If you play an acoustic instrument, or if you can sing, you are very welcome to come along and have a go. But don't forget that a non-playing audience is just as welcome as musicians. So if you fancy coming along to listen to what is on offer, give it a go, and enjoy.
