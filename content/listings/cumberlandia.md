A boutique folk festival with a maximum of 500 people on site, organised by a volunteer committee of musicians, artists, craftspeople, and hard workers, whose ambition is to put on a memorable weekend that shines a spotlight on local culture. The main days are Friday and Saturday, with Sunday a more relaxed day featuring a few performances around lunchtime and impromptu fireside sessions.
