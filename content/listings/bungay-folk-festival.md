Now in its third year. Poets, writers, artists, musicians and actors all live within close proximity of the town — Bungay has that effect on people. The programme includes sessions and concerts, featuring some well-known names. The festival is all about participation. Visitors are invited to enjoy the ambience, the eclectic and exciting shops, and the excellent food and beer. Visitors are encouraged to bring their own instruments.
