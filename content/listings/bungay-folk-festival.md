Now in its 3rd year. There are poets, writers, artists, musicians and actors all living within close proximity of the town. Bungay has that effect on people. Sessions and concerts, and some well-known names. The festival is all about participation. Visitors are invited to come and enjoy the ambience, the eclectic and exciting shops, and the excellent food and beer. Visitors are encouraged to bring their own instruments.
