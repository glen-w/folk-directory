The Lift is based in Glossop, Derbyshire celebrated its 10th anniversary in 2007. It operates as a collective and is one of the longest running entirely voluntary global music clubs in the UK. In the last 12 years we have put on over 200 concerts by top quality national and international performers. Our main aim is to celebrate the vitality of cultural diversity and this is reflected in our programme of regular concerts and annual festivals (see our archive page), together with occasional school and community based music workshops.

Where it happens: Lift events are planned in the front rooms of houses and the back rooms of pubs, and take place at a number of venues in the town including the Town Hall and the Globe Hotel.
