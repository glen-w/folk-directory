Cheshire Folk is a folk club in Middlewich, originally set up by Liz Rosenfield around 18 years ago. It takes place on the first Sunday of the month at the Boars Head Hotel, Kinderton St, Middlewich CW10 0JE, starting at 8pm. The evening is acoustic and spans the folk world both traditional and modern — song and story, poetry and tunes, singer-songwriters and instrumentalists are all welcome, regardless of standard, age, or experience. The format is sing-around style, hosted by Liz Rosenfield with occasional guest hosting by David Giles, her singing and guitar-playing partner in the duo Bitter and Twisted. A much-coveted Cheshire Folk Cup is awarded to a performer who has impressed during the evening.

The club welcomes regular visitors from Northwich Folk Club, Sandbach Folk Club, Timperley Folk Club, the Leather Smithy at Langley, and from North Wales, Staffordshire, and Manchester. Situated by the canal, it also attracts musical boaters and holiday-makers passing through Middlewich; the nearest mooring points are between the Kings Lock and the Big Lock, with a short walk to the Town Bridge where the Boars Head is visible to the right of the bridge, opposite the church.

Liz Rosenfield, a former director of the original Middlewich Folk and Boat Festival with experience at Cropredy, the Festival of Great Britain, Lift Off Festival, Warwick, and Chester, also organises Folk At The Lock, a canal-side mini festival at the King's Lock in Middlewich, running annually since August 2023. The festival schedule includes a concert on Friday night, a folk club stage on Saturday, a concert on Saturday night, folk and blues on Sunday afternoon, and a Cheshire Folk session on Sunday night. She also occasionally books well-known folk artists for small venues around Cheshire.
