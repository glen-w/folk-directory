Ryburn 3 Step is a folk development organisation promoting traditional song, music and dance in the Ryburn Valley, near Halifax, West Yorkshire. The organisation is based at Hollins Mill, Hollins Mill Lane, Sowerby Bridge, HX6 2QG, with additional sessions at 103 Oldham Road, Ripponden, HX6 4EB, a venue that helped establish the organisation.

Regular events include a folk club night on the second Sunday of each month, mostly featuring guest performers, at Hollins Mill; a Northern English tune session on Mondays at 8 pm at The Old Bridge Inn, Ripponden; a singing session on Thursdays from 7.30 pm at 103 Oldham Road; and Ryburn Longsword practice sessions on Wednesday evenings from 7 pm in the upstairs room at Hollins Mill. New dancers are welcome to the longsword sessions.

The Thursday singing session, known as Singing in the Shop, was revived in November 2024 and features a wide variety of traditional songs; it continues a weekly singing event previously run by Chris Coe. House concerts are also presented at 103 Oldham Road for touring artists with limited dates. Pete Coe, a professional folk musician of over 50 years' experience as singer, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, dance musician and caller, maintains a substantial sound and print library available as a resource for participants.
