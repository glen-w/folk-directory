Spurred on by a love of folk music and outdoor activity, and seeing potential in a farm not used to its full potential, 20–30 volunteers came together to begin a big tidy-up and clear-out. Through the efforts of countless volunteers and five festivals, the aim to renovate and maintain the farm has been a success. There is still work to be done.
