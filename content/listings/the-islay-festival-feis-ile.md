40th Anniversary ! Uses several locations on the island. Has it's own website now (previously we linked to a site which regularly talked about it and the island in general). Mixes folk and whisky on an island well-known for both. And it's a very pretty island. With Concerts and Ceilidhs, usually. Make sure you find your accommodation as it can be a busy event.
