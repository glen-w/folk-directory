TwickFolk presents live acoustic music most Sundays at The Cabbage Patch pub (Patchworks), two minutes from Twickenham Station. Operating in South West London since January 1983, the club is one of the best-known hosts of live acoustic, folk, blues and Americana in South East England, booking acts across a broad range of genres – traditional and contemporary music from Britain, Europe and beyond. Doors open at 7.45pm for music at 8pm. The Cabbage Patch serves real ales and craft beers alongside food.

TwickFolk is a registered charity (no. 1159059) and is affiliated to Folk21, a loose affiliation of folk clubs exploring new avenues for gaining audiences. Tickets are cheaper when purchased in advance via WeGotTickets. The club supports the Attitude is Everything Access Starts Online campaign, and its access information page has been approved by the campaign. A YouTube playlist of highlights from previous events is available.
