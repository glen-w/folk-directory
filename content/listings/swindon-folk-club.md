Swindon Folk Club aims to enjoy and promote folk music across the spectrum: vocal and instrumental, traditional and modern, music hall, blues, ragtime and country. The club welcomes performers of all abilities, and visitors are equally welcome to come and listen. The atmosphere is friendly and encouraging.

Founded by Ted and Ivy Poole and friends in 1960, the club (formerly Swindon Folksingers' Club) has a long history of keeping traditional music alive in this busy town in north-west Wiltshire. From its beginnings in the folk revival, the club has seen Swindon's character change from railway town to a modern centre of new technology and financial services; through it all, Swindon Folk Club has remained a friendly, relaxed and welcoming place where anyone can come and sing, play or listen.

The club meets on the third Friday of every month from 8:00 pm to 11:00 pm, upstairs at The Hop Inn, 8 Devizes Rd, Swindon SN1 4BH. Club nights are £5; guest nights vary. Swindon Folk Club is a not-for-profit organisation.
