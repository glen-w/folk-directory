Monthly singaround sessions take place on the second Monday of each month at Ye Olde Vic, 1 Chatham St, Edgeley, Stockport, SK3 9ED. The sessions are attended by former regulars of the Midway Folk Club, which ceased to exist in May 2022. The pub is advertised as closed on Mondays but opens specifically for the singaround.

The Ye Olde Vic Band runs an all-welcome English Tunes session every second, fourth, and fifth Wednesday, starting at 8.00 pm.

Ye Olde Vic is a traditional pub, first opened in 1877 as the 'Victoria Vaults' and also known as 'The Victoria'. It stocks a varying selection of real ales and opens Tuesday to Sunday from 5 pm to closing time. The pub has a real fire and a strict no-swearing policy.
