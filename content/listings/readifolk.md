Readifolk is a folk song club in the heart of Reading, running since 1982. Sessions take place on Sunday nights from 8 to 10.30pm.

Guest nights feature a set of floor singers followed by a set from the guest, then an interval with an opportunity to buy CDs and raffle tickets. After the interval there is a second set of floor singers and a second set from the guest. The charge for guest nights is £10 (£5 for under-25s).

Singers' nights are informal evenings where everyone is invited to sing or play a tune, though there is no pressure and visitors are welcome simply to listen. The charge for singers' nights is £3 (free for under-25s).

There is no bar at Readifolk, so visitors are asked to bring their own refreshments. A corner shop and a pub are just round the corner.
