Bracknell Folk Club meets on Tuesday evenings from 8:00 p.m. to 10:30 p.m. at The Foresters Arms, 173 London Rd., Bagshot GU19 5DH, on the A30 opposite Waitrose. The programme covers folk, blues, and roots music, with real ales available. Singers' nights are £3 and guest nights £8.
