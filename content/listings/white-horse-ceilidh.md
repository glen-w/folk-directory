The emphasis is more on having fun than getting it right or 'historically correct', so it doesn't matter if you've never done it before. The caller walks through every dance before it starts then prompts during the dance, you can't go wrong!

We're lucky to be supported by a number of excellent local bands. We also like to see ourselves as a stepping stone for up and coming young bands and callers who have often emerged from local sessions or dance teams, creating an exiting and unique atmosphere.
