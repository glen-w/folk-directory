Louth Folk Club meets at the Louth Jazz Club, 51 Queen Street, Louth, on the second and fourth Thursday of each month. Doors open at 7.30 pm and the session begins at 8 pm. Non-member entrance is £4, except on guest nights.

The programme alternates between club nights, half-sessions, and guest nights featuring visiting musicians. Floor singers are welcome at all events.
