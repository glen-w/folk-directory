The Otley Black Sheep Folk Festival is an established annual folk festival held in Otley over a weekend in September. The programme runs from Friday evening through to Sunday evening, offering a mix of larger concerts at the Parish Church and Otley Common alongside more intimate performances at the Black Horse and the Labour Rooms. Otley-based 'Folk For All' runs slower sessions for those new to playing. The festival features a mix of national and local artists, and camping is available on site. Weekend tickets are sold in advance.
