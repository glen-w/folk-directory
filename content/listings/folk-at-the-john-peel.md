Folk at the John Peel is a series of folk and traditional music concerts at the John Peel Theatre in Wigton, Cumbria. The venue is a small proscenium arch theatre with 90 raked seats, wheelchair access, and an induction loop. A licensed bar offers real coffee, ice cream, and snacks.

The theatre is the home of Wigton Theatre Club, a volunteer-run charity that has been presenting live shows in Wigton for over seventy years. Tickets are available online or by phone; the venue is cashless for ticket sales, though cash is accepted at the bar for refreshments. Booking in advance is recommended, with tickets also available on the door subject to availability.
