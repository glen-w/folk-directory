The Somers Traditional Folk Club is Worcester's longest-running folk club, meeting on the first and third (and fifth) Fridays of the month at Bishop Allenby Hall, St. Stephen's Church, off St. Stephen's Street, Worcester WR3 7HS. The hall is booked from 8:00 pm to 11:00 pm, with a social break during the evening. Entry is £3.00 for members and £3.50 for visitors; membership is £10 to 31 December and can be joined at the door.

The club is named after a Worcestershire pub where it originally met over 30 years ago. Traditional music in all its varieties is celebrated, but is by no means an exclusive requirement: choruses, ballads, and shanties from English, Scottish, Welsh, Irish, American, and French traditions are all welcome, alongside contemporary and self-penned songs, acoustic blues, spirituals, musical hall, and humorous numbers. Instrumental tunes on guitar, fiddle, harmonica, flute, whistle, and concertina are also heard. The club is friendly, relaxed, and encouraging, with no paid guests on club nights and no amplification. Performers are not expected to read from sheet music, and while the club is not a singaround session, choruses are joined in. On first Fridays, performers in the first half of the evening are asked to choose a song with a chorus.

Visitors are asked to bring their own drink and glasses and to take all rubbish home. No animals and no unaccompanied under-18s. Help with setting up and clearing away is gratefully welcomed. There is good parking in the surrounding churchyard. Those with symptoms of respiratory infection are asked not to attend.
