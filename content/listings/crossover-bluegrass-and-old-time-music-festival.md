A newish festival, it began in 2017 featuring American Oldtime and Bluegrass festival. So while not a pure folk festival, it has some America-style folk music. Worth a visit, as it is not exactly expensive especially if you just go for one day. And when it's big and famous, you can tell your kids you were there at the beginning.
