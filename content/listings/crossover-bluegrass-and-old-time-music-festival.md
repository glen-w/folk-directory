The Crossover Bluegrass and Old-Time Music Festival began in 2017. While not a purely folk event, the festival features American Old-Time and Bluegrass music. It is held at Sherbrooke Scout Camp Site, Calverton, on Friday 1st May. Entry is affordable, particularly for a single-day visit.
