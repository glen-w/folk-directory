The festival offers a friendly and relaxed celebration of live music and culture in the village of Priston, six miles from Bath. A free open-air stage features a line-up spanning traditional, jazz, world, blues, rock, classical, and choral music, alongside kids' events, morris dancing, singing and dance workshops, a children's art project, performance poetry, book talk, market stalls, and food and drink. Ticketed events are also staged in local venues over the weekend.
