An Lanntair Arts Centre is an arts venue in the Outer Hebrides with galleries, a performance stage, and a café bar. It hosts exhibitions, talks, live music, and festivals throughout the year. A membership scheme (Caraidean) offers perks and invitations to exclusive events. The café bar provides a space to relax in a welcoming, creative environment.
