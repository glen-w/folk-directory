A friendly club embracing all styles of acoustic music. Club nights are held monthly, normally on the fourth Friday, starting at 8.00pm. There is no membership; performing and non-performing visitors are welcome.

Guest nights are ticketed (WeGotTickets or via the club). Feature nights are £5 on the door, with floor spots in the first half; singers nights and PA nights are £2.50. There is no club night in August. Dates can vary, so check the website.
