Sidmouth Folk Festival is a week-long folk music festival held in Sidmouth, Devon, on the East Devon coast. Running each August (30 July–6 August in 2027), the festival has been held since 1955 and attracts tens of thousands of visitors. Over 800 events take place across the eight days.

The programme spans concerts, ceilidhs, workshops, late-night sessions, dance displays, and free performances and sessions. Family activities run daily, and the festival is open to all. A festival campsite is available on site, and tickets are offered as day, weekend, week, or individual event passes. The festival also supports a dedicated charity.
