Downend Folk & Roots is a concert series based at Christ Church Downend in South Gloucestershire, running since 2014. The programme features folk and roots music across the year, with each concert pairing a headline act with a support act. Genres span indie folk, alternative folk, traditional music, and ceilidh.

Concerts are typically held on Fridays, with doors opening at 7.00pm and the music starting around 7.45pm. Seating is unreserved. A bar is available, stocking cider, soft drinks, wine, hot drinks, and real ale from Bristol's Hop Union Brewery. Attendees are encouraged to bring their own glass or mug (a 50p discount applies) and reusable water bottles. Sweet treats are provided by Radstock-based The Great Cake Company, and a prize draw at each event helps fund the support artists.

Parking is available in a car park behind the Parish Hall/Medical Centre, with free on-street parking in the surrounding area; parking on the church grounds is not permitted unless agreed in advance. Season tickets covering the full programme are available.
