The evenings start at 8.30pm and finish by about 10.45. Performers are warmly welcome on singer's nights, with free entry on these evenings. There is a small stage, lights, and a built-in sound system to cope with most situations. Groups or performers travelling from a distance may wish to ring up to request an extended slot, depending on numbers on the evening. Guests are hosted each month (3rd Sunday).
