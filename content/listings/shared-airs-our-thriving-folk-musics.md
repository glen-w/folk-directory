A bit different - a museum display! The Royal College of Music Museum's special exhibition explores the world of contemporary folk traditions, celebrating how and why folk musics continue to evolve, live and thrive. Co-curated with folk musicians and researchers, a range of historical and new instruments such as the hardingfele, hurdy-gurdy and harps demonstrate the importance of folk music, dance
