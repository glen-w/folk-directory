Since 1972, Wath Festival has been all about great music, community spirit and having a good time. Now in its 54th year, the festival has hosted some of the biggest names in roots music. It hosts various events, with the weekend being the highlight. Wath Festival won 'Best Village Festival 2013' in Feata Magazine and was runner-up for 'Festival of the Year 2010' in Feata Magazine. The festival takes place in Wath upon Dearne, near Rotherham, on Friday 1st May.
