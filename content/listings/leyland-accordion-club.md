Leyland Accordion Club meet on the first, third and if there is one, the fifth Wednesday in each month. The first and fifth Wednesdays of the month are informal nights where we drink coffee, talk about music, play, give instruction etc, 

The third Wednesday is always our concert night where we hold a concert on the stage, this is similar to an open mic night at other clubs.  On a concert night the doors open at 7.30 PM. From 8pm onwards we listen to a variety of amateur and professional players on our stage. If you are a player, then bring your accordion along and give us a tune.
