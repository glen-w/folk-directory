Haverfolk, Romford HaverFolk meets in The Golden Lion, 2 High Street, Romford, Essex, RM1 1HR every Wednesday from 8pm to chucking out time. They run mainly floor spots, with anyone welcome to perform or listen, and are most encouraging to beginners.
