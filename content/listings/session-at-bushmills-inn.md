The Bushmills Inn Ballads & Tunes Session takes place every Saturday night in the Gas Bar at the Bushmills Inn, Main Street, Bushmills, Co. Antrim BT57 8QG, Northern Ireland. The session features traditional Irish and Scottish music of the region, along with a variety of ballads in which the audience is encouraged to join in. The mix of Irish and Scottish repertoire reflects the session's location on the north-east coast of Ireland, looking out across the sea to the Scottish Highlands and Islands.

The regular group of musicians includes Riley Piddington (lead vocalist, guitar, mandolin), Dick Glasgow (founding member; hammered dulcimer, fiddle, Anglo and English concertinas, tenor banjo, mandolin), Sabine Glasgow (harp, fife, crumhorn, vocals), Desi Hasson (congas, bodhrán, spoons, vocals), Brian Shaw (lead vocalist, ukulele, bodhrán, spoons), and Sam Fleming (Northumbrian pipes, harmonica, tin whistles). The range of instruments heard at the session also includes fiddle, harp, mandolin, Northumbrian pipes, hammered dulcimer, fife, English and Anglo concertinas, tenor banjo, harmonica, tin whistle, bodhrán, and often Scottish smallpipes.

Passing musicians and singers are welcome to join in. Extra sessions occasionally take place, particularly during the summer months when visiting musicians are in the area.
