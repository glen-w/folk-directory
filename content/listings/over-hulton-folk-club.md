Over Hulton Folk Club meets at the Over Hulton Conservative Club, St Helens Road, Over Hulton, Bolton, BL5 1AA. Concerts take place on the first Wednesday of each month and singarounds on the third Wednesday, beginning at 7.30pm. All are welcome to play, sing, recite poetry, or simply sit and listen.

The club promotes and celebrates folk music through live performances and community events. Singarounds offer a supportive and encouraging environment where anyone can join in the music-making or enjoy the sounds of others. The club is run by a small committee including Corrie, Brian, John, and Steve.
