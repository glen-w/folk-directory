Whether you're interested in music, dance, song or just want an enjoyable day out, the Malton & Norton Folk Festival is packed with a variety of concerts, workshops and displays designed to bring a colourful and joyful start to the festival season. 

There's a \"Meet and Greet\" session and sessions for singers and musicians. We also have workshops and have sensational display teams to give astunning show in the Market Place.

All the details can also be found on our website www.maltonfolk.co.uk or keep an eye out for us on Facebook.

Come and and join us for the fun and frolics of the Malton & Norton Folk Festival 2012.
