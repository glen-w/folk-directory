The Dragon Folk Club is a weekly singers' night held every Friday at 8:15 pm at The Bridge Inn, Shortwood, Bristol. Everyone is welcome, whether they sing, play, or simply listen. Entry is free, with a raffle held at the interval. Some sessions are given a theme (for example Harvest, Halloween, Bonfire Night, Remembrance, St Andrew's Day, or Christmas), while others are open and unthemed.
