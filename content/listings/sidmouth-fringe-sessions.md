A big fringe over four evenings, featuring a strong line-up. All events take place in McBuzz's Barn. The Fringe Sessions feature a mix of emerging talent alongside established favourites from across the UK and internationally. Entry is free for all music lovers, with a donations bucket for the artists. The music is an eclectic mix.
