Devoran Acoustic Sessions presents folk, roots and acoustic artists at Devoran Village Hall from autumn through to early summer. Usually monthly, but not on a set night. Devoran Village Hall is a Grade II listed building, originally built as a workshop for The Redruth and Chasewater Railway, and provides an intimate setting for live music.
