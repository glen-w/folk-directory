Moseley Folk & Arts Festival is a non-camping, family-friendly festival held in Moseley Park, an 11-acre woodland glade in the heart of Moseley Village, Birmingham. Although just two miles from the city centre, the site offers a world away from city life, with sloping grounds creating a natural amphitheatre in front of a lake.

The festival explores contemporary folk music alongside arts and crafts, presenting an intimate setting for a range of performers. It also hosts local gigs and events throughout the year in Birmingham, keeping the festival spirit alive between editions.
