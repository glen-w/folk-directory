Launceston Folk Club is a grassroots venue for folk, acoustic, blues, roots, and singer-songwriter music, based at The Old Police Station in Launceston. The club runs monthly concerts featuring nationally and internationally acclaimed musicians, and also showcases upcoming and young musicians drawn from across Cornwall and Devon, giving them the opportunity to perform support slots alongside established artists and to take part in showcase events.

Membership runs for 12 months from the date of joining, with an annual subscription, and offers a discount on tickets and other benefits. The club is sponsored by ALFCIC and receives grant support from Cornwall Council.
