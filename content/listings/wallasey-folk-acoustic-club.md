Wallasey Folk & Acoustic Club meets on the first Saturday of every month at Café UniTea, 100 Liscard Road, CH44 0AA. Doors open at 7pm, with a 7.30pm start and a finish by 10pm at the latest. Entry is free.

The evening is a singaround without a PA, and all are welcome to join in. Members are encouraged to bring their own songs, tunes and poems. Tea, coffee and cake are available, and visitors may bring their own drinks.

The club is run by a group of Wirral-based local volunteers in their own time. It is a not-for-profit community organisation and a member of the EFDSS. Those who need specific access support are asked to contact the club in advance, and those interested in helping out are welcome to get in touch.
