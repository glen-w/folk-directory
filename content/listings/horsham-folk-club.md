Horsham Folk Club is an acoustic folk club meeting Sunday evenings at 8 pm (doors open 7:45) at The Normandy Centre, Denne Road, Horsham, West Sussex RH12 1JF. The venue has a licensed bar. Admission usually varies between £4 and £10, with discounted rates for members; under-21s receive free admission to all events.

The programme includes open floor nights, where anyone may try a song, a tune, or a poem in front of a sympathetic audience (with no obligation to perform), as well as song-and-dance evenings featuring a ceilidh band and experienced callers. Guest performers are also booked regularly. Beginners and experts alike are welcome.
