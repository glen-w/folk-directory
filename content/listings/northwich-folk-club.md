Northwich Folk Club has run continuously since 1977, offering a mix of guest nights and singers nights with a friendly atmosphere and broad outlook. The club meets every Friday from 8:00 to just after 10:30 at The Royal British Legion (Social) Club, Firth Fields, Davenham, Northwich CW9 8JB.

Annual membership (January to December) is £10 and includes a discount on entry to all guest nights. Advance booking is recommended for guest nights (no booking fee) and can be made online or in person at the club. A 3-monthly A5 newsletter is available at the club or for download. On busy guest nights, overflow parking may be arranged at Davenham C of E Primary School; this is publicised for the specific event. Otherwise, parking inside the school gates is not permitted.
