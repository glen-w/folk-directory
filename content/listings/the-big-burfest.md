All the performers at the Burfest have kindly performed free of charge in support of the Village Hall centenary fund. Half of the profits and donations on the day will go directly to this fund, helping the Trustees ensure the continued enjoyment and improvement of the hall into the next century. The other half of the profits will go to the nominated charity.
