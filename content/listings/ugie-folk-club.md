The Ugie Folk Club is a small folk club based in Longside, Aberdeenshire, running for over 25 years. It has hosted a wide range of acts and bands and runs an annual Folk Festival. All players of all abilities are welcome — singers, instrumentalists, groups, reciters of poetry, and tellers of tales.

The club meets on the first Friday of every month (weather permitting) at the club house of the Longside Football Club.
