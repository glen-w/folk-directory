A folk club has been meeting in Mansfield for over 50 years. Its venue has changed many times, and since September 2021 the club is based at the Forest Town Arena on Clipstone Road West, Mansfield, where a warm welcome is extended to all.

The club meets every Monday evening throughout the year, including most Bank Holidays. Doors open at 7.00pm for a prompt 7.30pm start, with an aim to finish by 10.00pm. A varied programme runs across the year: acoustic Singer & Musician Nights open to whoever turns up, sometimes in traditional sing-around format; Feature Nights in which two 20-minute slots are given to a named performer, the rest of the evening being open to floor spots; and approximately monthly Guest Nights with a main artist and named support act(s). The programme is available to view and download as a PDF.

Admission is £1 for Singer & Musician Nights, £2 for Feature Nights, and £5 for Guest Nights. Free parking is available; visitors should register their car number plate on the tablet in the bar to avoid a penalty parking charge.
