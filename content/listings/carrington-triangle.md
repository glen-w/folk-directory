The Carrington Triangle is a small folk club in a parish setting, with an upstairs performance room and no PA, relying on intimate, unamplified delivery. The club holds regular nights featuring guest artists — solo performers, duos, and trios — alongside a large selection of floor singers and instrumentalists.

The musical range is broad, encompassing traditional and contemporary songwriting, Americana-influenced harmony singing, blues, and political song, with a strong emphasis on original material and audience participation. Guest artists are frequently booked for repeat visits, and the club is noted for its welcoming atmosphere and singalong-friendly audience.
