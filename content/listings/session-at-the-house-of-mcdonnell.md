The House of McDonnell is a traditional Irish pub at 71 Castle Street, Ballycastle, Co. Antrim, Northern Ireland, hosting a weekly Irish traditional music session every Friday night. Extra sessions are often arranged during the summer months when visiting musicians are in the area. Established in 1766, the pub is Grade A listed and retains its original interior dating from around 1870. It is described by CAMRA as "a great classic among Irish pubs" and appears on the National Inventory of Historic Pub Interiors. Locals know the bar as "Tom's," after host Tom O'Neill.

Instruments heard at the session typically include fiddle, melodeon, harp, mandolin, Anglo concertina, tenor banjo, tin whistle, bodhrán, and uilleann pipes. Musicians and singers passing through the Causeway Coast and Glens of Antrim are welcome to join in and share tunes and songs.

The venue was featured on the BBC's Irish music programme Blas Ceoil in November 2010, with performances by regular Friday-night musicians.
