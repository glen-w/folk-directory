The Watford Folk Club hosts regular sing-arounds, a well-known fixture within the local folk community. Evenings are friendly and welcoming, open to all who wish to share folk songs, tunes, poems, and stories, or simply come and listen. Sing-arounds start at 8pm, with entry by suggested donation.

The club also presents folk, roots, and acoustic music, featuring professional and semi-professional acts. The club is closed during the festival and holiday season, resuming with a sing-around in September.
