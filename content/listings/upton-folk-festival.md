The Upton-upon-Severn Folk Festival takes place in the town of Upton-upon-Severn (WR8 0HW), running from 30 April to 3 May. Tickets go on sale around 1 December at an earlybird price.

The programme includes concerts, ceilidhs, Morris and other dance display teams, stalls by the waterfront, a Sunday procession, folk clubs, music and song sessions, a musical meander, and an acoustic talent contest.
