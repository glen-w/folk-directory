Mid-week Music runs every Wednesday from 8.30 pm. Contact Nick for an advance floor spot. Musicians get three songs/15 mins or play for 30 mins if they are a main act. These include bands and solo artists. The night has a relaxed, friendly atmosphere. All are welcome!
