Devizes Folk Club presents traditional and contemporary folk music and song on Monday evenings at 8pm (except August and bank holidays) in the Lamb Inn, St John's Street, Devizes, Wiltshire. The club encourages singing, playing and performance, and has a local guest and a major guest each month. All other nights are singers' nights.
