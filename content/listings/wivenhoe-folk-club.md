The Wivenhoe Folk Club has been meeting at The Greyhound in Wivenhoe High Street since 5th March, 1992.

The Club is no-smoking and largely acoustic. 

The booked guests are nationally known and often internationally known artistes; floor spots by local and near-local performers are always welcomed.
