Nailsea Folk Club has been running since 1987 and maintains a strong reputation for its concert programme, featuring artists from around the world. Most concerts are held in Nailsea Tithe Barn, a 15th-century building with excellent acoustics and a comfortable setting; the club was instrumental in the barn's refurbishment. The venue is fully accessible for people with physical disabilities and has modern facilities. Some concerts are also held at Holy Trinity Church.

Every second Tuesday throughout the year at 8pm, the club holds its Singers and Musicians Nights at the Grove Sports Centre.

The club is also involved in fundraising events and supports local causes, including Memories at the Barn, which supports people living with dementia and their carers. Concerts often sell out, so booking in advance is advisable. Online tickets are subject to a 7.5% booking fee.
