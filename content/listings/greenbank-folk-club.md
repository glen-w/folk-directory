Greenbank Folk Club is a queer-led, alternative folk club in Bristol, dedicated to reviving the radical spirit of the 1960s and 70s folk revival and reimagining it for the communities shaping the city today. Inspired by the communal, DIY culture of early folk gatherings, the club embraces a radical inclusivity that celebrates the full spectrum of identities and experiences within Bristol's cultural landscape.

The club hosts regular sessions, concerts, workshops, and ceilidhs, creating a space where everyone is welcome to sing, listen, collaborate, learn, or simply belong. The aim is to honour tradition while opening doors to what folk music can become next.
