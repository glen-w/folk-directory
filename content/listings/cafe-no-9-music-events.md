Cafe No 9 is a small music venue at 9 Nether Edge Road, Sheffield (S7 1RU), with a capacity of around 40. The venue has disabled access but no disabled toilets.

The programme features regular evening shows, with doors at 7:00pm and the performance starting at 7:30pm. Line-ups span singer-songwriters, blues, folk, and storytelling, with occasional young songwriter showcases and special support acts.
