The club room is upstairs. The club welcomes all visitors but cannot safely provide assistance or be held liable for disabled access.

As of January 2025, doors open at 7:00 pm, music begins at 7:30 pm, the guest performs at 8:00 pm, and the evening finishes at 10:00 pm.

Membership is £5.00 per year for a single member and £8.00 per year for a couple or family. Cash (Sterling) only is accepted.
