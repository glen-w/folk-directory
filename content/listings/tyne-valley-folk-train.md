The Tyne Valley Folk Train is a folk music session held aboard a train journey from Newcastle Central station to The Bowes Hotel, a village pub in Bardon Mill. It runs a few times each year, offering a lively session of traditional and some more modern tunes and songs performed by local musicians while travelling through the Tyne Valley.

The organisation also hosts a FolknFell music weekend with fell walking, occasional gigs, and a "Day of Music" combining workshops, session playing, and a concert.

The Tyne Valley Folk Train was launched within the Festival of the North East in 2013. It is supported by Northern Rail, the Tyne Valley Community Rail Partnership, and The Bowes Hotel.
