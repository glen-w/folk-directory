A folk club in Wrexham since the mid-1960s, Wrexham Folk & Acoustic Club has continued with hardly a break, moving between venues in and around the town. Its current home, for over 15 years, is The Nag's Head on Mount Street, at the junction of Mount Street, Yorke Street and Tuttle Street, beside the tall chimney that was part of Soames Brewery (built 1894 as an expansion of the original Nag's Head Brewery, founded 1834). The club meets on the first and third Thursdays of each month from October through June, with a break on the first Thursday in January, and kicks off at 8.00 pm.

The resident band, Goff Jones (guitar and vocals) and Geoff Jones (cello and double bass), are both former members of Yardarm, the band long associated with folk music in the Wrexham and North Wales area. Geoff Jones played with Yardarm in 1972 for about 12 months and again in 1982–83. The band opens each evening before a guest act or floor singers perform. Events are run unplugged in the upstairs function room, noted for its intimate atmosphere and excellent acoustics, with a maximum capacity of 50. The pub offers good cask ales and food.

Guest nights, traditionally on the first Thursday (occasionally the third), feature acts from across the country and beyond. Singers' nights, usually on the third Thursday (occasionally the first), are open to anyone wishing to sing, play an instrument, or recite poetry or prose; admission is free, with a raffle and a pass-the-hat to fund guest nights. Previous guests have included Archie Fisher, Dave Cousins, Brian Willoughby, Catherine Craig, Prelude, Allan Taylor, Harvey Andrews, The Houghton Weavers, Huw Williams, Jez Lowe, Barbara Dixon, Fred Wedlock, Jeremy Taylor, The Ronstadt Generation, Santa Cruz River Band, Julie Felix, Martin Carthy, Rod Clements, Hughie Jones, The Oyster Band, Rab Noakes, Kieran Halpin, Winter Wilson, Paul Walker & Karen Pfeiffer, Jonny Dyer & Vicky Swan, Vera Aspey with Dave Howard of The Oldham Tinkers, Stanley Accrington, and Bernard Wrigley.
