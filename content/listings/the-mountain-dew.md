A Blackheath folk club. Acoustic open-mic night every Tuesday at The British Oak, 109 Old Dover Road, London SE3 8SU, featuring a mixture of floorspots and special guests. Starts at 8.30pm. Irish session usually on the second Friday of the month; the Christmas session will be Friday 5th December 2008.
