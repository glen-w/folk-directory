On the edge of the Cotswolds, minutes from the M40, Manor Farm is ideally situated for convenience in an environment for learning and enjoying the company of fellow fiddlers.

Workshops, masterclasses, concerts and a ceilidh. One-to-one tuition also available from some tutors.

Camping available for attendees and family - advance booking required.
