The Bristol Folk House is a cooperative venue at 40A Park Street, Bristol (BS1 5JG), hosting live music, festivals, dance and comedy nights on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays. The programme also includes courses, a darkroom, a flea market, a choir, and venue hire.

The box office is open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 5pm, and closed on Sundays. All gigs are seated with unreserved seating. Under-12s go free and under-16s must be accompanied by an adult. The café/bar opens from 7pm to 11pm and serves bar snacks in the evening. Tickets are non-refundable, and an admin fee is included in all ticket prices sold by the venue. The venue has no disabled access or facilities.

The Bristol Folk House sells tickets on behalf of organisers, promoters and artists; tickets may also be available from other outlets. A music programme is offered, and membership is available.
