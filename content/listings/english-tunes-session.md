The English tunes session meets on the second Tuesday of the month from 8pm to 10pm at Woodie's at the Junction Inn, Priory Road, Southampton SO17 2JZ. The repertoire covers English hornpipes, reels, jigs, and waltzes, drawing on sources including Playford's Dancing Master, White's 100 Popular Hornpipes, Reels, Jigs and Country Dances (1880), and the New Forest collections of Alice Gillington (1911).

The session has moved venues several times over the years, having previously been held at the Handle Bar in the Avenue, the Platform Tavern on Town Quay, and the Talking Heads on The Polygon. Sessions paused during the pandemic and resumed in May 2022. The current venue, Woodie's at the Junction, is the session's original home, reopened in a refurbished form in October 2023.

Visitors are welcome to join in playing.
