The Burnaby Arms is a folk music session held at the Wellington Arms, Wellington Street, Bedford (MK40 2JX), on the last Tuesday of each month from 7.30pm onwards. The group mostly plays English music at a steady pace, with occasional Northumbrian and continental tunes in the mix. All abilities are welcome, and anyone is free to join in.

A tunebook of core repertoire is available for players, though there is no obligation to play from it.
