The Hoy at Anchor Folk Club is one of the longest-running folk clubs in the country, having started in 1970 at the 'Hoy and Helmet' pub in Benfleet before moving to the nearby 'Anchor' pub and eventually settling at the Estuary Club in Leigh-on-Sea. The club meets every Tuesday evening at 7:45pm in the Estuary Club's function room, presenting national, international, and local folk musicians.

The vast majority of concerts are presented without amplification, whether a full band or an unaccompanied singer. As a non-profit organisation run entirely by volunteers, admission is typically under £10, with reduced rates for members who join for a nominal fee. The Estuary Club's bar is available to attendees at club prices.

Regular events also include a monthly folk tunes session (free to join or listen), a monthly singaround at the Mayflower in Old Leigh, and an Open Night offering floor spots for local folk talent.
