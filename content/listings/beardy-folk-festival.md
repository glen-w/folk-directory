Beardy Folk Festival is a new Midsummer music weekend featuring a line-up of contemporary, traditional and acoustic indie folk music. The festival site includes children's entertainment, a food court, real ale, cider and cocktail bars, circus workshops, traditional rides, a climbing wall, yoga, and a small trade village.
