Filey Folk Festival is one of the mainstays of this North Yorkshire seaside town's musical year. It is a free-entry independent folk festival on the North Yorkshire Coast, with ticketed evening concerts also available. Over 100 musicians perform Traditional and Contemporary Folk, Americana, Country, Blues, and everything in between. The festival also features Singarounds/Sessions.
