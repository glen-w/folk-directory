The Tenterden Folk Festival, organised by the Tenterden Folk Day Trust, last took place in October 2024. The trust continues to hold regular folk song and music sessions at The Vine Inn, Tenterden, along with occasional events including an all-day sing-a-round. The trust is a registered charity (No 1038663) promoting folk song, music, dance, crafts and traditions, and also maintains a free guide to folk events across Kent, Surrey, Sussex and beyond.
