HaverFolk - The Feelgood Folk Club!

We have an open session every Wednesday when you'll be most welcome to sing, play or simply listen to some great music in a friendly atmosphere.

On the first Wednesday of every month we have either a popular guest artist or band, or else a featured evening for one of our talented regulars, so, make it a date - Wednesday at eight!
