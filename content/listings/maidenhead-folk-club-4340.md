Maidenhead Folk Club meets every Thursday evening at Elizabeth House, Station Hill, Cookham, SL6 9BS. Doors open at 7.45pm and the singaround runs from 8pm until around 10.15pm. The nights are open to anyone wishing to perform in all kinds of acoustic styles, contemporary and traditional, and new faces are always welcome whether singing, playing, or simply listening and joining in a chorus.

There is no bar, so visitors are asked to bring their own drinks. A contribution of £5 per person covers the cost of venue hire for a singaround (£10 for a guest night). Some parking is available outside the venue (park diagonally, following the faint lines), with additional spaces in the public parking area for the shops and station next door.

The club takes a summer break, resuming in September. Guest nights feature named artists throughout the year.
