Haddington Folk Club is a long-established East Lothian club open to anyone with an interest in folk music, whether to listen or to contribute. Most evenings take the form of a singaround, where anyone present can have a go at showing off their musical achievements. Occasional guest nights feature professional folk musicians, and shorter spots are given to members.

The club meets at the Mercat Hotel, Haddington. Visitors are welcome; bring an instrument or just yourself.
