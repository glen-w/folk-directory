Forest Folk Club is a not-for-profit organisation promoting folk and acoustic music in the Forest of Dean, Gloucestershire, for over 35 years. The club meets at the Orepool Inn, Chepstow Road, Sling, Coleford GL16 8LH, a venue noted for its cosy atmosphere, good food, and ale.

The club runs guest nights and "Singers Nights," the latter welcoming musicians and performers of all acoustic music to showcase their talents. Doors open at 7.30pm for an 8pm start. Admission is cash only: £8 for guest nights and £2 for Singers Nights.
