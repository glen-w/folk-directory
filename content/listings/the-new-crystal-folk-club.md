The New Crystal Folk Club is a weekly folk club open to players and singers of all types of music, poets, storytellers, and listeners. It meets every Friday at 8.00pm at Hill & Cakemore Liberal Club, Halesowen B62 9JG, and is hosted by Dennis Gem.

Most Fridays are open performer nights, with the number of songs each person gets depending on how many performers attend. New performers are welcome. On guest evenings, floor spots are limited to invited regulars. The bar offers reasonably priced drinks and closes at 11pm. Admission is free except on guest evenings, and a raffle supports the running of the club.

The club was founded in August 2015 and moved to Hill & Cakemore Liberal Club in September 2021. The venue has been welcoming and supportive of the club.
