The Lyceum Folk & Acoustic Club meets every Thursday at 20:30 in the Function Room upstairs at the Crindau Constitutional Club, Malpas Road, Newport, South Wales, NP20 5PJ (entrance via Goodrich Crescent). Singer's Nights are held on weeks without guest acts, and virtual Zoomarounds take place on the third Tuesday of each month, also starting at 20:30.

The club welcomes all acoustic instruments and musical styles, from beginners to experienced players, and songs may be self-written or borrowed. Poetry is also welcome. Visitors are free to play or simply listen.
