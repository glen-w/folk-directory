Folklincs is a folk music organisation in Barton-upon-Humber, Lincolnshire, offering tuition, an acoustic music club, and slow sessions. The Saturday Traditional Music School is held at Baysgarth School, Barrow Road, Barton-upon-Humber, DN18 6AE. The Acoustic Music Club and Slow Sessions take place at The Corn Exchange, 8 Market Place, Barton-upon-Humber, DN18 5DA.

Tuition covers accordion, bodhrán, concertina, violin/fiddle, folk song, guitar, ukulele (beginner, all ages), melodeon, mandolin, penny whistle, bagpipes, and four-string banjo, with workshops open to all ages. Tutors are experienced musicians offering friendly, step-by-step instruction for beginners, improvers, and more experienced players.

The Acoustic Music Club provides a stage for live folk music performance, welcoming both performers and listeners. Slow Sessions focus on developing instrumental technique and building a folk music repertoire. Youthlincs, the youth strand of Folklincs, gives young musicians the opportunity to perform on stage.
