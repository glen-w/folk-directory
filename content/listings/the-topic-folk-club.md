The Topic Folk Club established in 1956 is the longest running and surviving folk club in the world. The club which meets on a Thursday at 20.30 books guests of all fields of folk music. The guests can be the well known names , but pride themselves in giving the lesser known artists a chance to perform at The Topic. The club also runs regular Singers and Musicians night usually on the first Thurday of the month however all Thurday nights in August are given to Singers nights
