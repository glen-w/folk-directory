An annual celebration in Portsoy of the town's rich maritime and cultural heritage. The festival features beautiful boats, exhilarating skiff races on the open sea, maritime and rural craft demonstrations, non-stop song and dance with an acoustic and Scottish flavour, mouth-watering food and drink, and adventure activities at the Sports Village (open to all ages).
