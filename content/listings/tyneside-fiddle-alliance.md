The Tyneside Fiddle Alliance is a community group of fiddle players founded in January 2011 by Stewart Hardy and David Jones of Bottle Bank Band. The group meets to play the music of North-East England, folk tunes from elsewhere in the UK, and traditions beyond these islands, with a repertoire ranging from the local music of James Hill to recent compositions from further afield.

Meetings take place on the first and third Thursdays of the month, plus any fifth Thursdays, from 6.30 to 8.30 pm at St George's Church Hall, Jesmond, Newcastle (NE2 2TF), where the group has been based since April 2023. The group operates an open-door, drop-in policy and welcomes fiddlers of all abilities from competent beginners upwards. The fee is £8 per adult, payable on the night, with under-18s free (an adult responsible for them must be present throughout) and reduced-fee options available. Music is provided and players are encouraged to play by ear; both accessible foundation lines and more challenging harmony lines are used.

The meetings are led by Stewart Hardy, David Jones, Beth Chamberlain, and Ali Say (a member since 2015), with special guest tutors invited from time to time. An informal session follows from around 8.45 to 10 pm in the same hall, with a requested charge of £3 to cover extra hall hire. The group also supports traditional music in the North East through festival participation, group competitions, and an annual concert in November.
