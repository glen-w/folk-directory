The club meets in a singaround format in the Church Hall at St Thomas More RC Church, 32 Field End Road, Eastcote, Pinner, Middx HA5 2QT. Parking is available on site. Meetings are usually on the first and third Wednesdays of each month (dates change occasionally; check the club's website to be sure). Doors open at 19.30 and music runs from 19.45 to 22.15.
