Set in the South Lanarkshire countryside close to the Borders, Clydesdale Folk Club is based in Biggar. Regular club nights are held on the last Thursday of every month, with various concerts hosted throughout the year. The programme features traditional and contemporary folk music and blues, with local, national and international artists and bands at affordable prices.

The club also hosts acoustic music nights, open to members and visitors alike, where attendees can play an instrument, sing a song, recite a poem or story, or simply enjoy the atmosphere. These take place upstairs in one of South Lanarkshire's best hotels and entrance is free.
