Central Bar Folk Club This folk club is held upstairs in the Central Bar, next to the Tyne Bridge on Hills Street, and meets on the 3rd Thursday of each month from 12.30 till 3.30pm. It's run in a sing around style, with some well known NE performers attending. The bar serves food as well as a selection of draught ales and spirits etc.
