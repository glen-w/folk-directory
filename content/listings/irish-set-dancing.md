Irish Set Dancing is a great way of socialising and keeping fit at the same time. It is a social form of dance where each dance is in the form of a square, danced with four couples. You don't need to bring a partner as many come just by themselves and pair up or swap with other dancers.
We dance to Irish music, usualy to CDs but occasionally to live music. We welcome all levels of dancers from beginners to the more experienced. We also have a tea/coffee break as we all like to have a chat and get to know each other better.
