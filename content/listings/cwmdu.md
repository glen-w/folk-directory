The Folk Club meets on the first Friday of each month at 8pm in Tafarn Cwmdu, a community-run pub in the village of Cwmdu. A core of regular local musicians gathers to play and sing folk music, with repertoire drawn from the Celtic countries, England, and across Europe over the course of an evening. Visitors are welcome and bring their own styles to the session.

By custom, one end of the room is set aside for those who simply wish to listen. The session dates back to the reopening of the pub nearly 30 years ago.
