Herga at The Castle is a folk and acoustic club meeting every Monday, with a few exceptions in the run-up to Christmas, at The Castle Public House, 30 West Street, Harrow on the Hill, HA1 3EF. Evenings start promptly at 8pm; arriving by 7:30 is recommended to allow time to park, find a seat, and buy a drink. Food is available at the pub on Monday nights, serving until 21:30.

The programme alternates between Come-All-Ye open evenings (free entry) and guest evenings (entry fee varies). Open evenings are led by a compere and open to anyone who wishes to play or sing, as well as those who prefer to sit back and enjoy the atmosphere. Raffle tickets are sold for a bottle of fine wine. No tickets are sold in advance for regular evenings.
