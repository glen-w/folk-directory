A loose collection of local talents, both music & song, mostly in the Folk tradition, with the odd bit of new or music hall thrown in for good measure.

Most sessions are a music & sing around, come all ye, based in a nice free house pub in Crowborough, with decent beer.

We meet on the 1st Saturday of every month, at 7.30pm ish.

You can catch us on Facebook as Folk Sessions & on Twitter @folksessions. If in doubt, give me a call - Dik Cook
