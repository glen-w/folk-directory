The Cutty Wren Folk Club meets at Redcar Borough Park Bowling Club, 1 Thwaites Lane, Redcar TS10 2FD. The club holds regular open "Singers & Musicians" nights alongside guest nights featuring visiting artists. A newsletter is published for members and visitors, and advance tickets for guest nights are available through We Got Tickets, with door tickets also available on the night if space permits.

The club is associated with local musicians including Paul Lucas, Alan Young, and Vince Parker, and has connections to other folk clubs in the area, including Guisborough Rugby Club Folk Club, The White Hart Folk Club, and Mickleby.
