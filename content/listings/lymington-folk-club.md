After many years, it has risen from the ashes in memory of local poet and songwriter Rod Legge.

It is hoped that it will become a focal point for local musicians to play, listen and share 'real' music in a friendly and welcoming atmosphere. Although called a 'Folk' club, it welcomes all styles of acoustic-based music.
