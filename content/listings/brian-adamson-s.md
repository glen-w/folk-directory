Brian Adamson's is a pub in Clones with a front bar and a larger back bar. The front bar hosts a live traditional Irish music session every Friday night, beside the fire. The back bar, with more space, typically features live bands on Saturday nights, and the venue can also accommodate private parties with food. A Wednesday-night open mic runs from approximately 9pm to 11pm, welcoming performers of any style. Sunday offers a quieter, more relaxed atmosphere.

The venue serves a range of drinks including tap beers, ciders, stout, craft beers, and a wide selection of whiskies, along with a pizza menu available for dine-in or pickup.
