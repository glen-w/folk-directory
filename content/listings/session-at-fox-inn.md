A session at the Fox Inn, Monkwood Green (WR6 2NX), runs on the last Friday of the month from around 8.30pm until late. Songs and tunes of any style are welcome. Traditional cider is served, and the venue can be busy and somewhat noisy.

To find the pub, go through Hallow, left following signs to Sinton Green, then left again at the next junction.
