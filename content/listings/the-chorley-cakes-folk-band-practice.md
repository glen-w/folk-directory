Chorley Cakes Folk is a community band that plays for ceilidhs, barn dances, concerts, and illustrated talks. Their music draws predominantly from the British Isles. The band has provided ceilidh music and callers for dances in and around Lancashire, and has also performed folk and traditional music at markets, festivals, and residential homes across the North West. They are available for hire for weddings, parties, fundraisers, and similar events.

The band runs a regular Ceilidh Club with callers, usually on the final Thursday of each month from September through to April. The venue is St Mary's Community Centre, Leyland, Lancashire, and the event runs from 7.30 to 10.30pm. Ceilidh Club evenings usually include floor spots from singers and dance teams, such as Galliard Folk Dancers.
