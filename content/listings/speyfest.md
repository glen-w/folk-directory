At the end of July, the playing fields on East Street in the Moray town of Fochabers will be home to the 23rd annual Speyfest, three days of the best in traditional and contemporary Celtic music, featuring concerts, ceilidhs and stomps. The Craft Fair and Festival Clubs are free to enter. The festival showcases some of the biggest names from the world of traditional and contemporary Celtic music. The festival takes place on Friday 24th July.
