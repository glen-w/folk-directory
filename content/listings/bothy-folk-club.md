Bothy Folk Club has been bringing folk music to Southport since 1965. The club meets Sunday nights at The Southport Bijou Cinema, Post Office Avenue, Southport PR9 0US. Doors open at 7pm for a 7.30pm start.

Programme nights alternate between national and international guest artists and 'Singers Nights', when the audience is invited to perform. A varied panel of resident performers supports the evening.

Tickets for guest nights are available in advance via We Got Tickets (a 10% or minimum 50p handling fee applies). Some guest nights sell out, but on most nights tickets are also available on the door.
