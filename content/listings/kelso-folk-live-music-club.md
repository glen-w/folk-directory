Kelso Folk & Live Music Club holds a traditional music session every Thursday from 8pm in the Saddlers' Bar at the Cross Keys. On Fridays, an acoustic floor spot for songs and tunes takes place at 7:45 in the Oak Room of the Cross Keys, followed by music with mics hosted by the variable line-up of The Cobbles Band at The Cobbles Inn from 10pm to midnight.

Visiting singers and players are welcomed at all three sessions. The club also hosts occasional ticketed events.
