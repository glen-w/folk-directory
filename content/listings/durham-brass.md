Something just a bit different. An explosion of bands and performers combining brass with folk, opera, dance and spectacular outdoor theatre descends on County Durham to fill some stunning venues and City streets with music and fantastic visual effects. New collaborations, digital trails, and established and emerging bands bring brass in every genre imaginable.
