Ebbw Vale Folk Club Ebbw Vale Folk club was reformed October 2010 and is held on the last Friday of the month at Ebbw Vale Welfare Club, Eugene Cross Park, Ebbw Vale, Gwent. The club has an “open mic” policy and is growing ever popular with at least a dozen regular participants. Jeff Jones and Mike "Rip" Harper are the people to contact. Welfare Tel.
