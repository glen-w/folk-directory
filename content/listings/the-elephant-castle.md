The Lewes Saturday Folk Club meets every Saturday evening from 7:30 to 10:30. The programme focuses mainly on British traditional music and song and contemporary folk music and song derived from the tradition. Entry is usually £5 for Come-All-Ye's, £6 for seasonal specials, and between £9 and £15 for guest nights. No membership is necessary, and floor singers and musicians are welcome.

Regular residents include Brad Chick, Carole Chick, Bryan Creer, Trevor Curry, Valmai Goodyear, Don Morgan, Sally Mounter, Milly Murphy, and Derek Seed. The club also runs a regular Zoom tunes session and offers workshops, with two half-price places per workshop available to under-25s.

The club meets at The Elephant & Castle in Lewes, though the venue is in the process of changing hands and the location may vary from week to week; visitors are advised to check ahead for the current location.
