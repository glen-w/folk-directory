The clubs activities can be split into 3 categories: Guest Nights, Singarounds and Musician Workshops / Sessions. Singarounds and guest nights start at 8.30pm. Singarounds are the very essence of what the club is about and are on the 2nd Monday of each month throughout the year (check the website for the January date if the 2nd Monday is Plough Monday).
