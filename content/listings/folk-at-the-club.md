Now in its fifth decade, Topsham Folk Club is one of the most vibrant and active folk clubs in the UK. Rooted in Topsham's rich history and unique seaport location, the club draws inspiration from a remarkable range of music and influences.

Monthly concerts take place on Sunday evenings at Matthews Hall, Topsham, from 8:00 pm to 10:30 pm, with doors opening at 7:30 pm. Tickets are available via singers' nights, the Matthews Hall café (card only), or online. Youth (under 26) concessions are available.

Singers' nights are held every Tuesday (apart from August), usually at the Globe Inn on Fore Street. The evening typically begins with tunes from 'the Orchestra' around 7:30 pm, with singers getting under way from 7:45 pm. Performers book a slot with the compere by 7:45 pm; the club does its best to give everyone a chance to take part. Spots are normally two songs or tunes in a ten-minute slot. Non-performers are welcome.

Instrumental sessions are typically held on the first Monday of each month at the Bridge Inn, Topsham, from 7:30 pm to 9:30 pm. Instrumentalists of all levels are warmly invited to join in.
