A village-based festival in the Forest of Dean with concerts, Morris dancing, a village fete, busking, poetry and storytelling, ceilidh dance, a songwriting competition, live comedy, and a folk walk on the Sunday. Held annually on the third Saturday in July. The 17th is acoustic only; the 18th includes PA systems.
