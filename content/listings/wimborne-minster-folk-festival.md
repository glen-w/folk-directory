A new group of Wimborne residents and business folk have come together to proceed with the mammoth task of organising the next festival, and so far it is full steam ahead with plans to continue the legendary festival! Traditional English and Celtic music and dance. Celidhs, Dance displays, children's entertainers, Market stalls. Three days of folk, family & fun last weekend in the summer sunshine!
