The Lamb Folk Club meets on the first and third Wednesdays of each month, upstairs in The Lamb Inn, High Street, Old Town, Eastbourne, BN21 1HH — the town's oldest pub, serving traditional Harvey's handpump and gravity real ales and good food. Doors open at 7.30pm, with the session starting at 8pm and finishing at 10.30pm.

The club presents a wide range of professional folk singers and players alongside floor spots for visiting musicians. On guest nights, some floor spots are available; singers and musicians are all welcome, though arriving early or advising in advance is recommended. Open stage nights carry no charge for floor singers, and admission is half price for students and under-21s.

The club is run by volunteers, with Nick Westcott and Nick Cant as residents.
