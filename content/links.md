If we have missed you or you would like to exchange links, please [contact us](/contact).

- [English Folk Info](https://www.englishfolkinfo.org.uk/regional.html) — directory of English folk clubs, sessions, dances and festivals
- [Folk Music in Wales](https://www.pluckandsqueeze.com/events.htm) — guide to Welsh folk events, clubs and links
- [Folk Music Map](https://folkmusicmap.wordpress.com/) — map-based guide to folk music clubs and venues
- [Kent Folk](https://www.kentfolk.com/) — Kent gig calendars and venue/musician listings
- [Living Tradition](https://www.livingtradition.co.uk/folkclubs/england) — folk club lists for England, Scotland, Wales, Ireland and elsewhere
- [Living Tradition — Ireland](https://www.livingtradition.co.uk/folkclubs-ireland) — folk club list for Ireland (2023 frozen)
- [Norfolk Folk](https://www.norfolkfolk.co.uk/) — Norfolk folk directory and diary of clubs, events and festivals
- [The Session](https://thesession.org/) — traditional music community with session listings, tunes and discussions
- [The Session — sessions](https://thesession.org/sessions/) — searchable traditional session listings
- [The Session — sessions map](https://thesession.org/sessions/map) — map of sessions
- [Sesh.ie — trad sessions map](https://sesh.ie/trad-map.html) — recurring trad sessions across Ireland
- [Sad Folk](https://www.sadfolk.co.uk/) — Somerset and Dorset folk diary of concerts, dances, sessions and workshops
- [Traditional Music](https://www.traditionalmusic.co.uk/folk-clubs/) — regional folk club pages covering Britain
- [UK Folk Festivals](https://www.ukfolkfestivals.co.uk/viewall.php) — directory of UK folk festivals
- [Wiltshire Folk Arts](https://www.wiltshirefolkarts.org.uk/) — Wiltshire traditional songs, music, customs and arts
